CREATE SHAREABLE REGION WINDOW — macOS INSTALLATION
===================================================

This is an unsigned, unnotarized application. Only continue if you downloaded
it from the project's official GitHub Releases page and trust the source.

INSTALL AND OPEN FOR THE FIRST TIME
-----------------------------------

1. Double-click CreateShareableRegionWindow-macOS.zip if your browser did not
   expand it automatically.
2. Open the resulting "Create Shareable Region Window" folder.
3. Drag CreateShareableRegionWindow.app into your Applications folder.
4. Open Applications and double-click CreateShareableRegionWindow. A warning
   will say that macOS cannot verify the developer or check the app for
   malicious software. THIS IS THE EXPECTED FIRST-LAUNCH STEP; the app will
   not open yet.
5. Click Done on that warning. Do not choose Move to Trash. After clicking
   Done, continue directly to the next step instead of trying to open the app
   again.
6. Choose Apple menu > System Settings > Privacy & Security.
7. Scroll down to the Security section and click Open Anyway next to the
   message about CreateShareableRegionWindow.
8. Authenticate if requested, then click Open in the confirmation dialog.
9. The app should now open. Grant Screen & System Audio Recording permission
   when macOS asks for it.

Apple makes Open Anyway available for about one hour after the blocked launch.
Once approved, the app can be opened normally from Applications.

SCREEN RECORDING PERMISSION
---------------------------

The app needs Screen Recording permission to capture the region you select.
Approve the request when prompted. If needed, go to System Settings > Privacy
& Security > Screen & System Audio Recording and enable
CreateShareableRegionWindow. Quit and reopen the app if macOS asks you to.

UPDATES AND UNINSTALLING
------------------------

To update, quit the app and replace the existing copy in Applications with the
new one. To uninstall, quit the app and move it from Applications to the Trash.

Apple's current instructions for opening an app from an unknown developer:
https://support.apple.com/guide/mac-help/open-a-mac-app-from-an-unknown-developer-mh40616/mac
